## Interview script (ask 5 to 8 people)

1. Think about your first month at a job. What was hardest about understanding the data or code you were given, and what did you do about it?
2. Did you use an AI tool to get up to speed? Did it ever give you an answer you later found out was wrong, or did you share anything you were unsure you should share?
3. If a tool shortened that first month, who would decide to pay for it, and roughly what would it need to save to be worth buying? Would you build it yourselves or buy it?
