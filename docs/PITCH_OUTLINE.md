# Pitch outline

Preliminary round: 5 minutes of pitch plus demo, then 4 minutes of Q and A. Final round: 8 plus 6. The demo counts inside the pitch time. Judges are told to prioritize clarity and credible answers over energy or slide polish.

## Five minute version

| Time | Section | What to say and show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:45 | Before | One new hire's first week. The dataset they were handed, why it is hard, our evidence (label findings versus assumptions) |
| 0:45 to 2:15 | Solution and demo | Point the app at the sample data, show the orientation, ask a question, show the sensitive value warning and the cited answer. Say what is Working, Simulated, Planned |
| 2:15 to 3:00 | After | What is measurably better: time to first useful question, fewer sensitive data mistakes. Label projections as assumptions |
| 3:00 to 4:30 | Venture case | User is the new hire, buyer is the employer onboarding junior hires. Bottom up sizing, cost per new hire from API usage, first pilot with a named cohort and a success measure |
| 4:30 to 5:00 | Disclosure and ask | Built at HackATL versus started with. What we need next |

## Questions to rehearse

Is this just ChatGPT with the data attached? Our answer: it is built for someone who does not yet know what to ask. It maps the data first, checks questions for sensitive values before sending, and cites every claim.

Why would a company buy this instead of building it? Our answer depends on what interviews tell us this weekend. Until then say plainly that larger companies may build their own and that smaller employers and fast growing teams are the first target.

What does not work yet? Say it first: sensitive detection is rule based and will miss things, answers can still be wrong, and the manager view is a mockup.
